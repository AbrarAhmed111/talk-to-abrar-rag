## What is PromptGraphy?
PromptGraphy is a unified generative AI creation suite that enables creators, marketers, and businesses to produce high-quality text, realistic images, and cinematic video from a single intuitive web interface.

## The Problem It Solves
Creators traditionally have to subscribe to multiple separate AI tools (one for text copywriting, another for image generation, and another for video rendering). PromptGraphy unifies these frontier models under one roof with a single credit pool and flexible plan tiers.

## What Abrar Ahmed Personally Built
Abrar engineered the entire web platform from the ground up:
1. **Multi-Model Orchestration:** Integrated leading generative AI APIs across OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, Flux image models, and Luma video generation.
2. **Dynamic Credit & Token Engine:** Architected an atomic usage-deduction engine that charges users variable credits depending on whether they generate text, high-resolution imagery, or intensive video renders.
3. **Monetization & Billing:** Integrated Stripe checkout, billing customer portal, and Stripe webhooks to manage automated upgrades, downgrades, and cancellations across Free, Lite, Plus, and Pro plans.
4. **Creator Editing Toolkit:** Built inpainting capabilities, background removal tools, and an embedded Pintura photo editing workflow directly in the browser.
5. **UI & Performance:** Crafted an ultra-responsive, accessible interface with comprehensive error boundaries, rate-limit notifications, and real-time generation feedback.
