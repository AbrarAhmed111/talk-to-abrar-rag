# Project: BizTradeHub — Online Business Marketplace

## Overview
- **Project Name:** BizTradeHub
- **Category:** Marketplace / B2B E-Commerce
- **Type:** Client Delivery
- **Live URL:** https://marketplace.vibanc.com/
- **Tech Stack:** Next.js, Python, FastAPI, MongoDB, REST APIs

## What is BizTradeHub?
BizTradeHub is a full-stack digital business acquisition marketplace (described conceptually as an "OLX for operating businesses"). It connects prospective buyers, investors, and business brokers with verified business listings across retail, digital products, franchises, and SaaS assets.

## The Problem It Solves
Buying or selling an established business is traditionally fragmented, relying on manual brokers and opaque paperwork. BizTradeHub provides a standardized platform with structured listings, financial metrics, and secure communication.

## What Abrar Ahmed Personally Built
Abrar engineered the full-stack system:
1. **Decoupled Architecture:** Built a high-speed Next.js frontend paired with a modular Python FastAPI backend and MongoDB database.
2. **Listing & Categorization Engine:** Created custom listing workflows with revenue details, valuations, industries, locations, and asset attachments.
3. **Discovery & Search:** Implemented multi-parameter search and filtration enabling buyers to pinpoint acquisition targets by price, cash flow, and industry.
4. **Secure Transactions:** Designed buyer-seller contact forms and negotiation flows.
